## The case

An estate. A locked door. A trail left by someone who knew he wouldn't be around to finish it.

The family thought they'd inherited a house and a bit of money. Then a message arrived — from him, sent to a schedule none of them knew about. The trail he left goes through Leeds, and it's yours to walk.

## What to expect

You'll receive messages on your phone throughout the experience. Each one takes you somewhere real in the city — a courtyard, a doorway, a name on a plaque — where the next piece is waiting. Some things the family already know. Some things they don't. You'll decide which is which.

It lasts around 90 to 120 minutes on foot. You can play any time within 90 days of booking. Once you start, the clock runs.
